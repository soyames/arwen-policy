Counterargument Set: Social Media Regulation

Argument: We support stricter regulation of social media platforms because they amplify misinformation during elections.
Evidence: Oxford Internet Institute found 68% of political misinformation originated from unverified accounts (2023 study).

Counterargument: However, excessive content moderation violates free speech and democratic discourse.
Evidence: According to the American Civil Liberties Union, over-blocking affected 22% of legitimate political speech in test cases (2024 report).

Alternative Approach: Implement transparent algorithmic auditing instead of direct content removal.
Evidence: Stanford's Algorithmic Transparency Project showed 40% reduction in harmful content with auditing alone (MIT 2024).
