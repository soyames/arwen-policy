Counterargument to Renewable Energy Policy:

While renewable energy mandates accelerate decarbonization, they impose significant economic costs on manufacturing sectors.
According to a National Bureau of Economics Analysis study (2022), industries face average 18% production cost increases when forced to source 100% renewable electricity immediately, potentially triggering job losses in critical supply chains.

Evidence: The policy would require upgrading legacy power grid infrastructure at estimated $420B cost, threatening competitiveness of US manufacturing against unfettered Chinese energy markets.
