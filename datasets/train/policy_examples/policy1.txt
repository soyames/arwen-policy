Policy on Renewable Energy Adoption:

Whereas existing energy mix relies heavily on fossil fuels,
We propose accelerated adoption of solar and wind energy technologies.

Because climate change projections show irreversible damage beyond 2030,
Therefore, all new government contracts for energy infrastructure must include renewable components.

The rationale for this policy is supported by recent MIT studies showing
85% cost reduction in solar panel production since 2015,
And Harvard research indicating 40% reduction in urban air pollution from wind farms.

Recommendation: Implement phased rollout with 50% renewable mandate by 2028.
